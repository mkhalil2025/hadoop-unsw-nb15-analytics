## 🎯 Presentation Overview

## 🎤 Presentation Usage Guidelines

### Before the Meeting
- Review client's website and business model
- Customize questions for their specific industry
- Prepare relevant examples and case studies
- Set up note-taking system for requirements gathering

### During the Presentation
- Focus on listening and understanding their current process
- Use the discovery questions as a framework, not a script
- Take detailed notes on pain points and requirements
- Demonstrate flexibility and customization capabilities

### After the Meeting
- Compile detailed requirements and pain points
- Create customized proposal based on their specific needs
- Schedule follow-up meeting to present tailored solution
- Begin technical planning based on their systems and volume

## 🔧 Customization Notes

This presentation framework can be adapted for different industries and business types:

- **Service Businesses**: Focus on recurring billing and client relationships
- **Product Companies**: Emphasize volume handling and payment processing
- **B2B Organizations**: Highlight professional communication and reporting
- **E-commerce**: Integration with shopping platforms and payment gateways
